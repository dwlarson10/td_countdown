Presentation nights: Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday

The Official Rules

  • Each couple selects a topic entirely of their own choosing.
  • The topic is classified. Sealed. Not to be revealed until the moment of presentation.
  • A PowerPoint shall be produced. Slides are mandatory. Effort is encouraged but not defined.
  • Length is at the discretion of the presenter. No minimum. Theoretically, no maximum.
  • Content may be serious, comic, educational, controversial, nostalgic, absurd, or some combination that defies easy categorization.
  • At the conclusion, the group votes. One winner. Eternal glory.
